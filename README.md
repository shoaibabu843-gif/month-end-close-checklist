# Monthly Bookkeeping Close Checklist (QuickBooks Online)

A repeatable month-end close for small businesses and real estate investors. Run it in the first week of each month for the month that just ended.

## 1. Get the data in

- [ ] All bank and credit card feeds are connected and up to date
- [ ] Every transaction in Bank transactions is matched or categorized, with its class or property
- [ ] Receipts and bills for the month are entered or attached
- [ ] Payment processor and rent platform deposits are recorded with their fees separated
- [ ] Owner contributions and distributions are recorded to equity, not to income or expense

## 2. Reconcile

- [ ] Every bank, credit card and savings account reconciled to the statement with a $0 difference
- [ ] Loan balances agree with the lender statements
- [ ] Tenant security deposits held agree with the security deposit bank account and the tenant list
- [ ] Mortgage escrow balance agrees with the lender's escrow statement
- [ ] Undeposited Funds is $0, apart from deposits in transit

## 3. Receivables and payables

- [ ] Review A/R Aging and follow up on late rent and unpaid invoices
- [ ] Record late fees allowed by the lease
- [ ] Review A/P Aging and make sure paid bills are marked paid

## 4. Real estate checks

- [ ] Each mortgage payment split into principal, interest and escrow
- [ ] Rent, deposits and expenses tagged to the right property
- [ ] Rehab costs on flips posted to Work in Progress, not to expenses
- [ ] Large purchases reviewed: repair (expense) or improvement (capitalize)

## 5. Adjusting entries (if your CPA wants monthly accruals)

- [ ] Depreciation and amortization for the month
- [ ] Prepaid insurance expensed for the month
- [ ] Rent received in advance moved to Prepaid Rent

## 6. Review

- [ ] Profit and Loss compared with last month and the same month last year, with big changes explained
- [ ] Profit and Loss by Class reviewed for each property
- [ ] Balance Sheet reviewed: every balance makes sense, no unexplained negative balances
- [ ] Uncategorized and Ask My Accountant are $0

## 7. Close and report

- [ ] Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow sent to the owner with a short plain-English summary
- [ ] Reconciliation reports saved
- [ ] Closing date set to the last day of the month
- [ ] Open questions listed for the owner
